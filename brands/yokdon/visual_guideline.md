# Visual Guideline: Yok Don — Intimate Wilderness

## Vibe

Intimate documentary wilderness: close to nature, quiet, observant, grounded, never glossy or touristy. The camera feels like a patient field reporter watching real life unfold inside Yok Don's dry dipterocarp forest.

## Lighting

- Natural low-key golden hour lighting.
- Motivated sunlight through forest canopy, morning mist, river reflection, or campfire glow.
- Soft rim light separates rangers, wildlife, or villagers from the background.
- Contrast is present but not harsh; shadows stay detailed.

## Color Palette

- Primary: Earth & Muted — dry ochre, muted moss green, warm soil brown.
- Highlights: soft golden amber.
- Shadows: slightly cool deep green or blue-gray.
- Avoid neon, glossy saturation, tourist postcard colors, and generic fantasy grading.

## Composition

- Vertical 9:16 first.
- Off-center rule of thirds.
- Layered foreground: tree trunks, branches, grass, smoke, dust, or riverbank objects.
- Keep important details inside a safe center crop for Shorts/Reels UI.
- Strong depth: foreground texture, clear subject, readable background.

## Lens

- 35mm documentary lens as default.
- f/1.8 to f/2.8, medium shallow depth of field.
- Natural bokeh, not artificial blur.

## Texture Signature

- Rough dipterocarp bark.
- Dry leaves and cracked soil.
- Serepok river reflections.
- Ranger fabric, canvas bags, dust, weathered surfaces.

## Negative Prompt

No text, captions, watermark, logo, UI, extra fingers, distorted faces, plastic skin, over-saturated colors, fantasy armor, tourist postcard look.
